Task Planner

Turn a long list of deadlines into a plan for today

Task Planner is for college students juggling assignments across several courses. Add your tasks, or bring them in from Canvas, and the app shows you what to work on first and helps you stay focused while you do it.

  • 📥 Import from Canvas Bring in all your upcoming assignments in one step, with hours estimated from the instructions.
  • 🎯 Smart priorities Every task is ranked by how much work is left, how soon it's due, and how much it counts toward your GPA, with the reason shown.
  • 📅 Clear deadlines Sort by priority or by due date, with countdowns that turn red when something is due soon.
  • ⏱ Focus mode A full-screen timer (Pomodoro, count up, or countdown) with piano music, and a record of time worked.

Getting started

  1. Click 📥 Import from Canvas, or type a task into the form.
  2. Open Courses and set each course's credits, so GPA impact is right.
  3. When you sit down to work, tap ▶ Focus on the task at the top.

🔒 No account needed. Your tasks are saved only in this browser on this device and never uploaded.

How do I get this file? (about 1 minute)
  1. Open your Canvas calendar: canvas.illinois.edu/calendar (or click Calendar in Canvas's left menu).
  2. At the bottom of the right-hand column, click Calendar Feed.
  3. A link appears. Copy it.
  4. Paste the link into a new browser tab's address bar and press Enter. A file ending in .ics downloads (usually to your Downloads folder).
  5. Come back here, click 📥 Import from Canvas, and pick that file.

🔒 Keep that link private: anyone who has it can see your Canvas calendar. The file stays on your device; this app never uploads it.
