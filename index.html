Getting started

  2. Enter your free time in 📋 Today's plan.
  3. Tap ▶ Focus on the first task and start.

🔒 No account needed. Tasks stay in this browser and are never uploaded.

How do I get this file? (about 1 minute)
  1. Sign in to your school's Canvas and click Calendar in the left menu.
  2. At the bottom of the right-hand column, click Calendar Feed.
  3. A link appears. Copy it.
  4. Paste the link into a new browser tab's address bar and press Enter. A file ending in .ics downloads (usually to your Downloads folder).
  5. Come back here, click 📥 Import from Canvas, and pick that file.

🔒 Keep that link private: anyone who has it can see your Canvas calendar. The file stays on your device; this app never uploads it.
